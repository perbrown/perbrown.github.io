With both sliders at 0 N, mass has no effect on fall time — a vacuum result Galileo predicted.
Each slider caps out at that object's own weight (Fg = mg), so at full resistance the upward force exactly cancels gravity and the object hovers. Since a given force produces less deceleration on a larger mass, the same newtons of resistance always slow Object A more than Object B.
