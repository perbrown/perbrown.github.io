      How This Works

      • + Table draws one table — click and drag on the grid. + Tables creates a whole grid of tables at once (pick rows & columns).
      • + Chair — click the canvas to place a chair; it snaps to the nearest table edge.
      • Drag a chair to move it, drag a table to move it (its chairs come along), or drag a table's corner handle to resize it.
      • Double-click a chair or table to delete it, or drag either onto the trash can (bottom-left).
      • Import Student List — paste your roster as Last, First, one per line.
      • Drag a name from the Student Roster onto an empty chair to seat that student.
      • Randomize — choose how many students should sit at each table, and every table is filled automatically. Anyone left over is lined up at the bottom.
      • Keep Apart Groups — drag students into a group; students in the same group will never be seated at the same table during Randomize.
      • Unseat All clears every seat but keeps the tables and chairs in place.
      • Export / Import Classroom saves your whole layout — tables, chairs, roster, and groups — to a file, or loads one back in.
      • Print Classroom / Print By Table toggle a print-friendly layout; then print from your browser's own Print menu (Ctrl/Cmd+P).

      Everything runs locally in your browser — tap the 🔒 privacy button for details.

      Your Privacy

      Your seating chart data is never saved or sent anywhere. Tables, chairs, your roster, and groups exist only in this browser tab's memory — nothing is uploaded to a server or stored in the cloud. Closing or reloading the page clears everything unless you use "Export Classroom" to save your own copy to a file.

      This site does use Google Analytics to collect anonymous visit statistics (like page views and general location) — that's standard site-traffic tracking, separate from your seating chart data, and it never includes anything you type or build here.
