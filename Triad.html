How to play

Every card has four features: number (1, 2 or 3), shape (circle, diamond, triangle), colour (coral, teal, indigo) and fill (solid, striped, outline).

A set is three cards where each feature is either all the same or all different across the three cards. If two cards share a feature and the third doesn't, it isn't a set.

  • Click three cards to claim a set. Correct sets are replaced from the deck.
  • When the table has no set, three more cards are dealt automatically.
  • The game ends when the deck is empty and no set remains.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter or Space to select. Press H for a hint.
